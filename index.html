What I build

Two halves of the same problem.

Regulated teams get told to choose between using AI and staying defensible. Both products exist so that isn't a choice: keep the data where it belongs, and keep a record of every consequential thing the machine did.

Product · onenew.ai

OneNew

A fully managed, private AI workspace that runs on your own cloud. Built for healthcare, finance, and legal teams that want a modern AI workspace without sending sensitive data to a shared AI provider.

Product · actpass.org

ActPass

Permission checks for AI agents. Every consequential action — a refund, a deploy, an outbound email — gets a deterministic allow, deny, or needs-approval decision before it executes, recorded in a tamper-evident evidence ledger.

About

I'm Akhilesh (Aki) Gogikar — founder and EVP of Mendel Info Labs.

I go by Aki professionally. My books and papers carry my full name, Akhilesh Gogikar — same person, and this page exists partly so you don't have to wonder.

I lead product and engineering across OneNew and ActPass. Most of what I think about sits at the seam between the two: how do you give a team real AI capability, and still be able to answer an auditor's question about what happened six months ago?

I write about compliance-first AI, agent security, and dynamical systems. If you're working on any of that — or you're a regulated team trying to figure out what's actually safe to deploy — I'm easy to reach.

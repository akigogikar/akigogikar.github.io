What I build

Controlled outcomes, not component catalogs.

Privacy-aware organizations need more than a public-model subscription. They need a private environment designed around approved data, workflows, policies, and people — delivered as one coherent customer experience.

Private AI services · onenew.ai

OneNew

OneNew is the single customer-facing offer for private AI built around each organization. Its service model is designed to discover the requirement, design the controlled solution, connect approved data, provision a managed private-AI Workspace on customer-controlled infrastructure, and operate it with the customer. Its broader direction spans Workspace, API, Desktop, organizational memory, private model access, and approved custom tools.

Independent governance · actpass.org

ActPass

ActPass is a separate, vendor-neutral governance product for AI agents. It evaluates consequential actions as allow, deny, or needs approval before execution.

About

I'm Akhilesh (Aki) Gogikar — founder and EVP of Mendel Info Labs.

I go by Aki professionally. My books and papers carry my full name, Akhilesh Gogikar — same person, and this page exists partly so you don't have to wonder.

I lead product and engineering across OneNew and ActPass. My work asks how to give privacy-aware organizations tailored AI capability while keeping their data, consequential actions, and decision evidence under meaningful human control.

I write about private AI systems, agent security, organizational memory, efficient inference, and dynamical systems. I aim to make difficult technical and governance choices easier to discuss, test, and defend. If you're working on any of that, I'm easy to reach and happy to compare notes.

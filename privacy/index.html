Privacy & analytics

Last updated: September 8, 2026.

Your choice

This website uses optional Google Analytics 4 to understand visits and improve its content. The Google analytics script loads only after you accept analytics. Rejecting does not restrict access to the website. Change your choice below or through Analytics settings in the footer.

What is measured

When you consent, Google receives pageview and standard usage information, such as browser/device information and visit timing. Our configuration supplies known page paths and referring origins without URL queries or fragments. We do not configure form-content capture, user IDs, advertising personalization, Google Signals or remarketing. Your browser connects to Google, which receives network information including your IP address; GA4 states it does not log or store individual IP addresses.

Analytics cookies identify a browser for measurement, not your name. We configure a 60-day cookie lifetime. A first-party local-storage record remembers your accept/reject choice for up to 180 days. If browser storage is unavailable, the choice may need to be made again on another page.

Withdrawal and service providers

Rejecting later disables further collection by this installation, clears its accessible first-party Analytics cookies and reloads the page to unload the Google script. It does not retract data already sent. GitHub Pages hosts this website and may process connection information independently of optional analytics. Google processes consented analytics data under its applicable terms.

Read Google's privacy policy, GA4 data privacy information and GitHub's privacy statement.
